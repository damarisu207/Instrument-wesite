How to play

  1. Tap or click any block in the paintings.
  2. Every block is its own instrument.
  3. Confetti pops out in the color of the block you touched.
  4. Tap quickly and mix blocks to make your own song.

Who plays what

  • Outer red square: marimba
  • Middle square: cello
  • Burgundy square: low cello
  • Red block: bell
  • White blocks: piano and harp
  • Blue block: drum
  • Cream block: flute
  • Yellow block: xylophone
  • Grey frame: soft pad

Sound turns on with your first tap. Turn your volume up!
